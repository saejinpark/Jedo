Jedo (제도) — App Review Notes

1. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Jedo is a hand-drafting desk for iPad. Real-size rulers, set squares, a parallel rule, templates, French curves and a compass lie on a sheet of drawing paper; the fingers hold and move the tools and Apple Pencil draws along their edges like a pencil. It is a practice tool, not CAD: there is no zoom, no dimension input and no automatic shapes, so that a centimetre on screen is a centimetre on paper.
- Audience: architecture and engineering drawing students, illustrators and comic artists who draw by hand, and anyone who wants to practise manual drafting. It is a general consumer app, not built for a specific business or organization.

2. SETUP AND HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
No login, account, sample file or purchase is needed. The app is free and has no in-app purchases. Apple Pencil is required to draw (fingers move the tools; a finger stroke on the paper shows a hint instead of drawing).
- On first launch the app asks which hand draws (오른손 = right, 왼손 = left) and shows three short cards; tap 다음 (next) and 시작하기 (start).
- Pick a paper size (e.g. "화면 맞춤" = fit to screen).
- Draw with Apple Pencil. For a right-handed layout the pencil choices are along the top right: lead sizes 0.3–0.9, wood pencil, technical pen, G and maru dip pens; grades 4H–6B below them.
- Top left: export as PNG (system share sheet), the arrangement menu (right/left hand, move groups, edge rulers, first-run cards), "새 종이" (new paper, with a confirmation), and "?".
- "?" explains the app: tap "?", then tap any control to read what it is and how it is used; tap "?" again to leave. This is the quickest way to review every feature.
- Bottom left, one row: undo/redo; the eraser, its kind (and the erasing shield), the compass, the knife; the layers list; then the desk tools (parallel rule, ruler, set squares, triangular scale, protractor, pins, templates, French curves, hip curve, tape measure). Tap a tool, then press a finger where it should lie; move it with one finger, turn it with two; draw along its edge with Apple Pencil.
- The row above it lays sheets over the drawing: tracing paper, writing papers, screen tone, a photo or PDF page, carbon paper; and holds the stylus and roller used with carbon. Tap a sheet to select it and see what can be done with it.
- Compass: tap the compass button, then hold a finger still on the paper for half a second to plant the needle, and draw around it with Apple Pencil.
- Knife: with a sheet lying, draw across it to cut it in two, or round in a loop to cut that shape out; laid flat on screen tone it scrapes the tone away.
- The map and stick at the bottom left move the view over paper larger than the screen. There is no zoom by design.
- The drawing is saved automatically on the device and restored on the next launch.

3. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. The app makes no network connections and uses no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising, payment or AI services. The drawing and a local log of pencil strokes (used to tune the feel of the tools) stay in the app's container on the device. No personal data is collected.

4. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same in all regions. The interface is in Korean.

5. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. Interface icons are Apple SF Symbols or drawn by the developer; tool shapes, the app icon and paper textures were made by the developer. The French curve outlines derive from a public-domain drawing on Wikimedia Commons (Krzywiki.svg). Photos and PDFs are only those the user picks; they stay on the device.
