Jedo (제도) — App Review Notes

1. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Jedo is a hand-drafting desk for iPad. Real-size rulers, set squares, a parallel rule, templates, French curves and a compass lie on a sheet of drawing paper; the fingers hold and move the tools and Apple Pencil draws along their edges like a pencil. It is a practice tool, not CAD: there is no zoom, no dimension input and no automatic shapes, so that a centimetre on screen is a centimetre on paper.
- Audience: architecture and engineering drawing students, illustrators and comic artists who draw by hand, and anyone who wants to practise manual drafting. It is a general consumer app, not built for a specific business or organization.

2. SETUP AND HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
No login, account, sample file or purchase is needed. The app is free and has no in-app purchases. Apple Pencil is required to draw (fingers move the tools; a finger stroke on the paper shows a hint instead of drawing).
- On launch, pick a paper size (e.g. "화면 맞춤" = fit to screen).
- Draw with Apple Pencil. Pencil choices are at the bottom left: lead sizes 0.3–0.9 and a wood pencil; grades 4H–6B below them.
- Bottom right row: tap the ruler or set square buttons to lay a tool on the paper. Move it with one finger, turn it with two. Draw along its edge with Apple Pencil and the line follows the edge.
- Bottom left: the compass button. Then hold a finger still on the paper for half a second to plant the needle, and draw around it with Apple Pencil.
- Layers button (stack icon, bottom right): lay tracing paper, carbon paper or screen tone over the drawing. When tone is present, a knife button appears to cut it.
- Top right: edge rulers on/off, export as PNG (system share sheet), and "새 종이" (new paper, with a confirmation).
- The drawing is saved automatically on the device and restored on the next launch.

3. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. The app makes no network connections and uses no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising, payment or AI services. The drawing and a local log of pencil strokes (used to tune the feel of the tools) stay in the app's container on the device. No personal data is collected.

4. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same in all regions. The interface is in Korean.

5. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. All interface icons are Apple SF Symbols; tool shapes, the app icon and paper textures were made by the developer.
